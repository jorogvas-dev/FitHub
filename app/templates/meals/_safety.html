Allergen declarations need independent review.

Unknown metadata is not “allergen-free”. When allergy restrictions apply, the planner excludes recipes with missing recipe or ingredient reviews and matching declared allergens. Ingredient data does not establish cross-contact safety.

Unwanted ingredients match your comma-separated ingredient labels exactly, ignoring case. Include all constituents and use consistent names. Dietary tags are separately declared; the planner does not infer them from nutrients.
